## Who can create a group in passbolt?

Only a passbolt administrator can create groups. When creating a group, the administrator
selects the initial members and must appoint at least one group manager.

## What is the difference between a group manager and group member?

The group manager is a group member who can add and remove members of the group, and
promote other members to group manager. No more, no less.

It is possible for a group member to share a resource they own in "read-only" mode with the
group. Group managers don't have extra rights to edit resource ownership.

## How do I add or remove group members?

Only group managers can add members to a group. To manage the members of a group where you
have the group manager role:

1. Select &#9881; > **Manage Users & Groups** in the top right corner
2. In the **Groups** section of the sidebar, right-click on the group, or hover over it and
   click its **...** button, then select **Edit**
3. In the **Edit group** dialog, add people using the search field, change a member's role
   between **Member** and **Group manager**, or remove members
4. Click **Save**

Multiple group managers can be assigned, and every group must keep at least one: passbolt
will not let you remove or demote the last group manager.

## What can an administrator do in a group they do not manage?

Administrators can rename a group, delete it, remove members, and change members' roles
between **Member** and **Group manager**, all without being a member of the group.

What the Admin role does not grant is **adding** members. Adding someone to a group means
decrypting each secret shared with the group and re-encrypting it for the newcomer, and
only someone with access to those secrets can do that. For an administrator who is not a
group manager, the **Edit group** dialog hides the add field and shows "Only the group
manager can add new people to a group."

See [roles and permissions](/admin/user-provisioning/roles-and-permissions/) for the full
comparison of the two roles.

## How do I recover a group whose only manager is unavailable?

If the only group manager of a group has been suspended or has left the organisation, the
group can no longer gain new members: a suspended manager cannot sign in, and
administrators cannot add members. To recover:

- **If the group has other members**, an administrator can promote one of them: open the
  **Edit group** dialog, change the member's role to **Group manager** and save. The
  unavailable manager can then be demoted or removed the same way.
- **If the unavailable manager is the only member**, there is nobody to promote. Either
  reinstate the manager (untick **Suspend this user** in their
  [user settings](/admin/user-provisioning/manage-users/)), or delete their account:
  deleting a user also deletes the groups in which they were the only member. You can also
  delete the group and create a new one.

:::caution
Both deletions are destructive: deleting the user or the group also deletes any passwords
that were only accessible through them. Reinstating the manager, even briefly, is the only
option that preserves everything. If you delete, do it from the user or group deletion
dialog so passbolt can prompt you to transfer ownership of shared items first.
:::

Deleting a user who is the sole manager of a group with other members prompts you to
transfer the group manager role during the deletion, see
[how to delete users](/admin/user-provisioning/delete-user/).
